Key Ingredients in NutriDerma Guard

Three carefully selected ingredients that work together.

Salicylic Acid

A beta hydroxy acid (BHA) that functions as a keratolytic agent — meaning it breaks down the bond between dead skin cells. It is commonly used in wart removal and acne treatments for its ability to decompose hardened, thickened skin layers and promote measured shedding [citation:1][citation:17].

Tea Tree Oil (Melaleuca Alternifolia)

A recognized essential oil often incorporated for its cleansing and clarifying effects on the skin. It has a substantial history in external preparations targeted at skin prone to imperfections, where it may assist in preserving a cleaner surface area [citation:17].

Castor Oil (Ricinus Communis)

A dense, botanical oil frequently utilized for its moisturizing and sealing qualities. It can assist in softening toughened skin, promoting moisture equilibrium, and forming a layer that helps other active agents remain on the skin's surface for an extended duration [citation:17].
